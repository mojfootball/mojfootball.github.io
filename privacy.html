Privacy Policy

Moj Football ("the app") is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Moj Football YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube upload permission of the channel owner's own Google account solely to upload the channel's videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user. Access tokens are kept only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
